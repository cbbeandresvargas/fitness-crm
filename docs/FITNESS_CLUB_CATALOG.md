# FITNESS_CLUB_CATALOG.md — Fitness Club Pass

## Memberships and Activities

Fitness Club Pass provides access to a network of affiliated gyms, sports facilities, and recreational activities through a digital membership and check-ins in the Fitness Club app.

Activity and center availability depends on the current partner network and membership conditions.

### Básica — Bs 290/month
- 20 monthly check-ins.
- Gym and weight training.
- Swimming.
- Boulder and climbing.
- Calisthenics.
- Boxing and kickboxing.
- Zumba.
- Spinning.
- Taekwondo.
- CrossFit.

### Pro — Bs 420/month
Includes everything in Básica, plus:
- Yoga.
- Pilates.

Pro has specific access limits for certain premium activities, such as yoga and pilates.

### Élite — Bs 560/month
Includes everything in Pro, plus:
- 30 monthly check-ins.
- Higher access limits for selected premium activities, such as yoga and pilates, compared with Pro.
- Exclusive activities coming soon. These activities have not yet been defined.

## Important Rules for the Sales Agent

- The total monthly check-in allowance is separate from the individual access limits for premium activities.
- Never invent premium activity quotas or promise unlimited access.
- Do not advertise specific Élite-exclusive activities until they have been officially defined.
- Verify current activity and partner availability before making specific promises.
- If a prospect asks about prices, answer clearly. When useful, first ask which activities interest them to provide a more relevant recommendation.
- Recommend a membership based on the prospect's interests and expected usage.

## Spanish Conversation Examples

**When a prospect asks about prices:**

"¡Claro! 😊 Para orientarte mejor, ¿qué actividades te interesan o te gustaría practicar? Tenemos opciones como gym, natación, pilates, CrossFit y otras actividades deportivas y recreativas."

**When a prospect is interested in gym and swimming:**

"¡Buenísimo! 😊 Si te interesan natación y gym, Fitness Club Pass te permite combinar diferentes actividades y centros afiliados con una sola membresía."

**When explaining the difference between Pro and Élite:**

"Pro incluye las actividades de Básica más yoga y pilates. Élite te permite acceder más veces a determinadas actividades premium y próximamente tendrá actividades exclusivas. 😊"
